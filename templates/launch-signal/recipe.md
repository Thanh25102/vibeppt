# Launch Signal recipe

Open with the market or workflow change, not the product name. Reveal the product only after the audience understands the tension. Give the product moment the largest visual area, then translate it into three or four concrete benefits. Use sourced metrics or operational evidence before the close. End with one pilot, meeting, or buying action.

Prefer short titles, cinematic images without embedded text, and a single dominant idea per slide. Do not invent customer outcomes or performance numbers.
